ACOUSTIC DRUM KIT
Kick, snare and hi-hat MP3 samples from Tone.js Audio:
https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/drum-samples/acoustic-kit
The collection credits Chris Wilson's Web Audio Samples:
https://github.com/cwilso/web-audio-samples
Source collection README:
https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/blob/master/drum-samples/README.md
The MP3 files are redistributed without audio edits. Playback adds variable
velocity, small pitch changes, stereo positioning and room processing.

ACOUSTIC GRAND PIANO
Salamander Grand Piano, Yamaha C5 stereo recordings by Alexander Holm.
Distribution: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Original collection notes: SALAMANDER-README.txt
Changes: 13 stereo notes extracted from the distributed MP3 set, limited
to eight seconds with a 200 ms fade-out, and re-encoded as MP3. Playback
adds transposition, mild equalization, note release and stereo room sound.
