PULSE AND RHYTHM / LESSON 1

Find the pulse.

Practise keeping a steady beat while the rhythm around you changes.

Back to the course outline

1 · Listen

Four evenly spaced beats

Listen to the clicks. They repeat at the same interval. That is the pulse.

1234

2 · Try

Clap or play along

Clap once per click, or play the same note on each beat.

1234

3 · Check

Keep the pulse under a new rhythm

A brighter sound plays between some of the beats. Keep clapping along with the lower clicks.

1234
