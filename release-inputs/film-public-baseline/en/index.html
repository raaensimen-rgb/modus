A PERSONAL WELCOME

m.

From the founder of MODUS

Welcome to MODUS.

Music is more than learning which notes to play. It is about understanding why they work together.

MODUS grew out of my own independent studies in music theory, guitar and composition. Along the way, I discovered how much easier learning becomes when theory, ear training and practical playing connect.

So I wanted to create a place where you can explore music at your own pace – whether you are just starting out or want to go deeper into harmony, improvisation and composition.

Here you can read, listen, experiment and build understanding step by step. My goal is not just for you to learn more theory, but to discover what you can do with music when you understand it.

Read more about the idea behind MODUS ↗︎
